University Viva & Evaluation Quick Guide
Examination Reference
Accuracy

Definition: Percentage of total predictions that were correct.

Formula: (TP + TN) / Total. High overall rate, but can be misleading in imbalanced datasets without checking recall.
Precision

Definition: Of customers predicted to churn, how many actually churned.

Formula: TP / (TP + FP). Measures cost-efficiency of marketing intervention to prevent wasting incentives.
Recall (Sensitivity)

Definition: Of customers who actually churned, how many the model identified.

Formula: TP / (TP + FN). Crucial in churn problems to ensure high-value defecting accounts are not missed.
F1-Score

Definition: The harmonic mean balancing precision and recall.

Formula: 2 * (Precision * Recall) / (Precision + Recall). Provides a single unified metric under class imbalance.
ROC-AUC

Definition: Measures how well the model separates churners from non-churners across all thresholds.

A score of 1.0 represents perfect discrimination, 0.50 denotes random guessing. Our score of {{ metadata.roc_auc }} indicates robust predictive separation.
