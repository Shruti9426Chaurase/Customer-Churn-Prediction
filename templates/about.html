Problem Statement

Customer acquisition costs (CAC) typically exceed retention costs by 5 to 7 times in subscription business models. Subscriber attrition degrades predictable recurring revenue and customer lifetime value (LTV). Identifying early warning signs and executing proactive, targeted customer success outreach before contract termination is vital to sustainable business growth.

Primary Objectives
  • Accurately forecast individual customer churn likelihood using calibrated probabilistic classifiers.
  • Assign standardized multi-tier risk categories (Low, Medium, High) with 0-100 risk scoring.
  • Provide transparent feature attribution explaining which observed factors correlate with the churn score.
  • Provide actionable, prioritized retention plays (e.g. contract migration incentives, technical support packages).
  • Enable interactive What-If scenario simulations for marketing strategy planning.
Dataset Specification

Trained on the standardized IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset containing 7,043 subscriber records and 21 attributes encompassing demographic profiles, subscribed services, account tenure, contractual commitments, and billing modalities.

Algorithms Evaluated
  • Logistic Regression: Linear baseline with class weighting to handle class imbalance.
  • Random Forest: Non-linear ensemble of decision trees mitigating overfitting via bagging.
  • Gradient Boosting: Sequential ensemble optimizing residual pseudo-loss gradients.

Genuine Technical Limitations
  • Data Stationarity: Assumes subscriber behavior patterns remain stationary; real-world shifts require automated scheduled retraining.
  • Correlation vs Causation: Observable factor weights reflect statistical correlations in historical cohorts and should not be misconstrued as absolute causal mechanisms.
  • Cross-Selling Telemetry: Dataset lacks app engagement frequency, live ticket sentiment, or latency telemetry.
Future Engineering Scope
  • Automated Retraining CI/CD: Scheduled data drift detection and model retraining with automated shadow deployments.
  • Enterprise CRM Integrations: Real-time webhooks into Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zendesk for automated ticket escalation.
  • Multi-Arm Bandit A/B Testing: Experimenting with competing retention discount offers to measure true causal intervention uplift.
  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLV): Joint modeling of churn risk alongside revenue potential for value-weighted prioritization.
